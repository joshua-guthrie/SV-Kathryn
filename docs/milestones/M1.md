## Modification → source comparison → correction

### A. Continuous cavity and support integration

The initial full-length loft of variable-edge offset wires was too slow/unstable.
The accepted inner envelope uses fixed-topology polygons sampled from true
inward-offset section boundaries. Interior chords retain material at the chine;
the exterior curve geometry is not replaced by this approximation. Actual-solid
tests check empty spaces and material thickness throughout the boat.

The original landing and mast end could no longer be treated as embedded in a
large filled body. They now bear on modeled members with joining contacts to
the real shell. The cabin sole level respects both the source-derived drop and
the actual bottom clearance.

### B. Source-coordinate migration

`parts/datum.py` reads the M0 registration ledger. Hull line stations, the three
hatches and mast-at-deck intersection now use its canonical coordinates.
Cabin edges, helm, winder, engine and traveller use located sheet-4 points.
Inherited fine bow/rail/davit details transfer by hull-station correspondence;
this is not a claim that every old fitting was newly dimensioned from the source.

The 50-ft recorded dimension remains the reference, not an assumed bound for
every projected piece. Current planked-body limits are **F -1.800 to 50.733 ft**,
a projected span of **52.533 ft** (500.38 mm at 1:32). The old 476.25-mm value
describes the 50-ft reference, not this complete projected body span.

### C. Gap found by full-section comparison: missing sheer band

Initial source overlays exposed a legacy reference error: the source's offset
table “Sheer” follows the **underside of the sheer strake**, while the separately
labelled **Top of Deck** is higher and wider. The old model used the former as
the deck edge, reducing freeboard and usable depth.

`drawing_package/sheer_band_measurements.json` retains original-pixel curve
picks and source hash. Seven measured profiles give approximately **12.5–16.1 in
of rise** and **1.9–4.5 in of outward flare per side**; endpoint closures are
explicit reconstruction choices. `checks/measure_sheer_band.py` reproduces them;
`renders/m1-sheer-band-source.png` shows the picks on the labelled source curves.

The actual shell/deck now include this upper band, while the source offset
readings remain unchanged. Deck-dependent fittings move with the corrected
reference. The lower rudder blade remains at its established underbody level;
the longer straight stock still enters the raised wheel box at 25 degrees.

The correction improved representative calibrated shell-guide RMS from
**4.31 → 1.13 in** on S06-V01 and **2.45 → 0.88 in** on S07-V02.
The inferred representative planes are not certified source frame-number
locations. Their vertical registration and keel/shoe reference differences are
saved explicitly in `checks/m1_section_comparison.json` for M2/M3 review.
