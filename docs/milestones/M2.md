## Review findings and corrections

1. **Continuous cut:** an exterior board under an uncut keel would not represent
   a functioning trunk. All stacked centreline material now shares one real slot.
2. **Bearing integration:** the former mast pad and filled-hull assumptions were
   replaced with a real mortised forward keelson, sole relief and partition cuts.
3. **Travel:** the initial -17-degree board trial intersected the main-hatch aft
   beam/deck. A -15-degree displayed pose and three intermediate positions clear
   actual fixed members. This is not proof of the boat's full historic stowage.
4. **Hoop seating:** the first raked hoop dipped below the horizontal mast heel
   into the mortise floor. Raising its lower plane above the whole raked heel
   removes that collision while retaining the iron foot fitting.
5. **Runtime:** dense ruled timber lofts and broad repeated whole-hull Boolean
   tests were too expensive; an automatic build hit 600 s. Smooth trunk lofts
   and adaptive keelson sections reduce unnecessary faces. The final 55-section
   keelson base differs from the hull rabbet by at most **0.0562 in**, checked at
   4,001 positions. Final full build **379.7 s**; no raised timeout limit.
6. **Shoe location:** moved the local widening from inherited coordinates to the
   complete source-located board slot. Actual pivot-plane shoe section is 10 in.
