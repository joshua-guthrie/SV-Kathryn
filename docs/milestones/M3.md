## Modification → review → correction

1. **Registration:** ruled out using lines-station or deck-beam numbers as hull
   frame IDs. Separate plan/caption/scarf checks establish the schedule above.
2. **Single timbers:** sequential section trimming and corrected primary taper
   endpoints remove disconnected fragments. All registered members pass the
   single-valid-solid guard.
3. **Original/repair interfaces:** actual primary tenons cut the keelson sides;
   repair-pattern timbers remain uninletted. The underpass cut also restores the
   bottom planking below F19/F20, rather than leaving the old keelson-seat hole.
4. **Fine bow seating:** independently offsetting the last frame left a small
   gap from the actual loft. Its profile now comes from the real cavity section;
   all bottom members have proven shell contact.
5. **Clamp integration:** moved main clamps inboard onto frame heads and
   extended their runs; oak reinforcement is separate. Hatch regressions now
   check real clamp/frame bearing rather than obsolete clamp/filler contact.
6. **Duplicate header:** first stanchion was nearly coincident with the existing
   after-hatch fore header. It now bears on that member, avoiding a duplicate beam.
7. **Samson/bowsprit:** source-locating the post exposed an inherited bowsprit
   heel extending aft through it. S04 deck/profile evidence locates the actual
   heel at the stem head. The post keeps its full shaft/head, seats into the
   lower stem knee, and clears the corrected spar. Its heel chock and new
   cross-timber have real headrail/chock/spar support contacts.
8. **Source section review:** held scale and frame F fixed. Only the independently
   named local vertical references were fitted. This avoids sliding the frames
   longitudinally to obtain a better-looking match.
