# Checked construction notes

## S02-METHOD

Seventeen field stations were placed irregularly between railway blocks, closer at bow and stern; the keel bottom centreline was the primary datum. The drawings were replotted into ten-section format.

Source views: S02-N01.

## S02-CORRECTION

Lines are based on a corrected/straight keel. The existing keel is dashed in the sheer plan. Stern sag about 8 inches and midship hollow deadrise about 3 inches port/2 inches starboard were left out of the lines for clarity.

Source views: S02-N01.

## S02-UNCERTAINTY

Stated accuracy of field notes is plus/minus 1/8 inch.

Source views: S02-N01.

## S05-05

Ceiling timbers: 1-3/4-inch pine. Forward timbers end at the chain locker; stern timbers end at the stern frame. Limber boards along the keelson are not present in the main hold. Ceiling members were removed by the recording team to inspect frames.

Source views: S05-N02.

## S05-06

Clamp: 5-1/2 x 2-3/4-inch pine; reinforced forward with 5-1/2 x 2-3/4-inch oak.

Source views: S05-N02.

## S05-07

Deck beams are keyed in reference to the bow. Members to either side of hatches/cabin are counted as a set. Beams 1–3 are 6 x 4-3/4-inch pine. Beams 4–23 average 5-1/2-inch square, plus/minus 1/2 inch, pine except 12–15 oak under winders. Stern timbers aft of rudder box are 4 x 3-1/2-inch oak except member d, 6 x 3-inch pine.

Source views: S05-N04.

## S05-08

Deck planks are mostly fir with some pine replacements, parallel to centreline, ends tapered to waterway. Thickness 1-3/4 inches; typical widths 3-1/2 to 4 inches, some inboard members 5 to 7 inches.

Source views: S05-N03.

## S05-10

Frames are oak; dimensions and pairing are highly irregular with old and new timbers. Twenty sets exposed from bow, inaccessible under main cabin; two more aft of main cabin. The printed note says first set against STERN knee. Sets 2–18 mortised into keelson; sets 19/20 pass under keelson. Preserve the printed wording; frame numbering must be interpreted separately from deck-beam numbering.

Source views: S05-N03.

## S05-11

Hull strakes predominantly oak at/below chine; pine replacement noted under ceiling aft of main cabin. Above-chine strakes are pine.

Source views: S05-N03.

## S05-12

Oak keelson, aft section inaccessible. Forward of main cabin it has two sections scarfed between frame sets 15 and 20. Aft section 1 foot 2 inches wide; forward section 1 foot 4 inches wide x 10-1/2 inches at garboard, tapered at bow.

Source views: S05-N03.

## S05-13

Knight heads: 11 x 4-inch oak.

Source views: S05-N03.

## S05-16

## S05-18

Source views: S05-N03.

## S05-27

## S05-FASTENINGS

Drawing wood names refer to white oak, pine and fir. Keelson sections fastened with drift pins; all hull fastenings observed by the HAER team were nails.

Source views: S05-N05.
