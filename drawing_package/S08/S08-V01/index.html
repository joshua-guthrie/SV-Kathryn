NOTES: Sails are made of dacron with nylon
stitching. The jib had a built-in draft of about
six inches at the foot. The main sail had no k,
discernible draft. The mast hoops and jib hanks a %
are not part of KATHRYN's sail rig. They are
shown for demonstration only.

SAIL DIMENSIONS:

Foot Leech Luff (6) f

Main: 49'-4" 68'-0" 45'-6" ;
Jib: 25-8" 42-0" 49-6" 5
Reef Point. b

Reefing Grommet: Grommets at the head,
clew and tack are of similar construction.
Jackline: Passes though lower mast hoops
and grommets, made off to the boom.
Jackline Grommets: Grommets at main foot
are of similar construction.

Jib Hanks: Seized with tarred marlin.
Mast Hoops: Seized with tarred marlin
above the jackline.
