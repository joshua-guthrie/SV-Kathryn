# Stern / helm correction before M4

> **Superseded preliminary plan.** The local rake/helm correction did not resolve
> overall stern fidelity. The current governing review and detailed remedy is
> **`STERN_REVIEW_AND_REMEDY_SPEC.md`**, with arrowed CAD/reference images.
> The stern is not accepted; M4 and the remaining milestones are on hold by
> the user's latest instruction. This file is retained as review history.

User observation: the stern seems to extend too far aft of the helm. Review the
measured drawings and the vessel-specific Library of Congress photographs,
correct supported discrepancies, then continue the next milestone (M4).

## Initial finding

The concern is supported. The current M3 body uses a vertical, flat transom
section at the farthest aft deck reference. S02's sheer plan and S04's inboard
profile show a strongly raked lower counter/transom; S04's deck plan and LOC
photos 28/30 show curved plan rounding. Applying the aftmost coordinate to the
whole transom leaves excess material at lower heights and square stern corners.
The reconstructed helm box is also only 1.7 ft fore/aft; the drawing footprint
is visibly longer. These are separate shape/size errors; the recorded-length
datum must not be shortened indiscriminately.

## Correction sequence

1. Retain source/native-pixel witnesses for upper deck edge, lower counter,
   plan curvature, box aft/fore faces, wheel and steering-stock position.
   Cross-check S02, S04 and photographs; photography does not supply an
   uncalibrated absolute length.
2. Correct the transom's rake and plan rounding while preserving supported
   deck-length/offset stations and the fair aft keel run.
3. Keep real normal planking thickness on the corrected transom: trim both
   exterior and cavity, not just cut a hole through the existing hollow shell.
4. Correct helm-box dimensions/placement to measured bounds, preserving a
   credible wheel/shaft/stock connection. Avoid moving the stock to disguise
   a shell or box error.
5. Refit transom frames, braces, aft clamps/rails and related deck parts to the
   new boundary; verify actual contacts, hull closure, stock passage and steering.
6. Inspect matched plan/profile/transverse views and compare LOC 28–30.
   Close this correction, then resume M4 boards and complete deck integration.
