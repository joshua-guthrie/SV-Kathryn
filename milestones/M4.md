## First increment: deck runs

The continuous deck material is partitioned into **109 single-solid segments
in 53 longitudinal runs**, replacing the registered aggregate rather than adding
seam lines over a backing slab. Thickness, camber, openings and the accepted
stern boundary come from the existing source-checked material envelope.

Stock follows S05 note 8: 1¾-in thickness, typical 3½–4-in widths (3¾ modeled),
and broader 5–7-in inboard stock (6-in centre run). Most deck stock is fir with
some pine replacements; exact replacement-board identities are not established.
Kingplank wood species remains undetermined as recorded on S06.

`checks/prototype_deck_boards.py` validates the actual partition before live
registration. High-accuracy source volume 90.7714590355 ft³; board volume
90.7714653399 ft³; discrepancy **0.0695 parts per million**. No gaps or backing
material are introduced. The 93.38-second prototype has 1,931 faces.

`checks/check_registered_deck.py` compares actual export bounds/volume with the
pre-partition assembly and checks every deck face has viewer triangles.
The final650-part build succeeds without warnings. Actual export validation
passes: all109 solids, no backing, **0.000-mm** change in union bounds and no
missing board mesh faces. Final images were inspected. Full stopping-point
handoff: `PASSDOWN_2026-11-11.md`.

## Second increment: source deck beams and stern reclassification

`drawing_package/m4_deck_schedule.json` now records the reconciled **23 S05-07
sets**, and `parts/beam_layout.py` + `parts/deck_beams.py` register **29 beam
parts**: 17 full/header beams and 12 half beams (six sets split around openings).
`checks/check_m4_beams.py` validates the schedule, stock, roles, stanchions,
stern timbers and the actual GLB export.

The split sets were proved from the drawing itself: at each picked edge the beam
line is absent across the clear opening width. Sets **8/9** split at the main hold
hatch, **16** at the after hatch, and **18/19/20** at the cabin. Sets 5/6, 7/10,
15, 17 and 21 are the opening-side headers. The after hatch has **no separate aft
header set**; its carlings bear on the cabin fore header (set 17), as anticipated
in the previous checkpoint. Header build stations were snapped by at most the
±0.20-ft pick allowance only where needed to keep the measured openings clear;
both the source pick and build station remain in the ledger.

Stock follows S05-07: sets 1–3 are 6×4¾-in pine (4¾ in sided); sets 4–23 are 5½
square pine except **12–15 oak under the winders**. The three hold stanchions land
on sets 15/14/13 within 0.06 ft, so no extra stanchion beam was invented. A
functional centreboard clearance notch keeps the fully raised board clear; the
beam section off-centre remains 5½ in square.

Stern reconciliation: the old `Stern oak deck timber` picks at preview **X182/220
are the measured beam sets 23/22**, now registered with the source pine stock. The
full transverse timber at the rudder-box aft face remains the modelled 4×3½-in oak
stern timber. The 7(c) leader also touches a transverse line near **F≈−1.05**, but
that line lies in the solid counter/transom edge and outside the interior air, so
it cannot be a standard deck beam and is retained only as an unresolved source
line. Member **d** (6×3-in pine) is not lettered on the plan and remains
unresolved; no third timber was invented.

Final build: **659 parts**, 564.9 s, no warnings. Actual export: all 29 beam parts
and all beam faces are present in the GLB; the 109 deck boards retain unchanged
bounds/volume and no backing. `check_m4_beams.py`, `check_m3_frames.py`,
`check_hatches.py`, `check_m1_enclosure.py`, `check_m2_centreline.py`,
`check_companionway.py`, `check_stern_remedy.py`, `check_stern_helm.py`,
`check_outer_integration.py`, `check_mast_mesh.py` and `check_model_scale.py`
pass. The starboard hydraulic ram hose was rerouted clear of the moving rudder
stock; the aft rail posts were confirmed to bear on the stern moulding.
