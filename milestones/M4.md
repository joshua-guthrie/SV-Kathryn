## First increment: deck runs

The continuous deck material is partitioned into **109 single-solid segments
in 53 longitudinal runs**, replacing the registered aggregate rather than adding
seam lines over a backing slab. Thickness, camber, openings and the accepted
stern boundary come from the existing source-checked material envelope.

Stock follows S05 note 8: 1¾-in thickness, typical 3½–4-in widths (3¾ modeled),
and broader 5–7-in inboard stock (6-in centre run). Most deck stock is fir with
some pine replacements; exact replacement-board identities are not established.
Kingplank wood species remains undetermined as recorded on S06.

`checks/prototype_deck_boards.py` validates the actual partition before live
registration. High-accuracy source volume 90.7714590355 ft³; board volume
90.7714653399 ft³; discrepancy **0.0695 parts per million**. No gaps or backing
material are introduced. The 93.38-second prototype has 1,931 faces.

`checks/check_registered_deck.py` compares actual export bounds/volume with the
pre-partition assembly and checks every deck face has viewer triangles.
Current build/export validation must finish before this increment is accepted.

## Provisional measured beam schedule — reconcile before use

`drawing_package/m4_deck_schedule.json` records **23 candidate deck-beam sets** from
S05-V01 timber edge pairs, retaining original-pixel coordinates, source hashes
and ±0.20-ft pick allowance. This is **not an active geometry input**.
`checks/measure_m4_layout.py` reproduces the ledger
and `renders/m4-deck-beam-source.png` labels the reviewed positions.

These numbers are distinct from hull-frame IDs and lines stations. First three
sets are 6×4¾-in pine; plan widths support 4¾ in sided and 6 in molded. Sets 4–23
use the printed 5½-in square average; sets **12–15 are oak under the winders**.
The first-three orientation and final set identification need independent
confirmation before integration. In particular, candidate sets 22/23 at preview
X220/182 coincide with existing members registered as stern oak timbers. Resolve
that classification, the printed 23-set count and member d before changing stock
or adding beams. The accepted stern geometry has not been altered for these picks.
