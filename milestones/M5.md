## Interior containment correction — 2026-11-13

The user markup showed the transom fore-aft frames/braces protruding below the
stern. The stiles had been trimmed to the filled outer hull, not the actual
interior cavity. They and all 49 cabin fit-out members are now intersected with
`hull.continuous_interior()`. `checks/check_interior_containment.py` verifies
**49 cabin + 6 transom members with zero volume outside the cavity**. Build:
841 parts, no warnings; M3/M5/M1/companionway regressions pass. Corrected
inspection: `renders/interior-containment.png`.
