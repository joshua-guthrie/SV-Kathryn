## 1. Executive finding

**The user's concern is supported: the overall stern still does not agree sufficiently with the references.** The earlier change improved the counter rake and helm-box footprint, but it did not complete the stern reconstruction.

The remaining issue is **more than one incorrect distance**:

1. The aft deck has a broad, nearly straight-backed slab and pronounced corner projections outside the curved rail. The current model projects approximately **17″ aft of the rail centreline on centreline, 22″ at 2′6″ off centreline, and 32″ near the quarter**. These are measured model projections, not approved target clearances.
2. The counter is generated by one planar cut anchored to an inherited aft coordinate. With the existing S04 review guides and a fixed local deck anchor, it lies approximately **12–13″ aft of those guides**. The guide identities and surveyed/corrected datum relationship must be confirmed before turning that apparent offset into a final construction target.
3. The rear rails are **fourteen fused round segments per rail**, whereas the drawings and photos show fair, rectangular-section timber rails. The transition into the side rails and the post arrangement also require retracing.
4. The helm has an improved plan footprint, but its lower front access opening is missing, and its hydraulic mechanism is represented only by shaft/bearing primitives.
5. Important stern-identifying features are absent or placeholders: the main-sheet traveller, the external pushboat bumper, the braced davit assemblies and local control equipment.
6. The substantial transom moulding and the local stern framing must be distinguished from thin deck planking, general rubbing strakes and projected fittings.

**Recommended approach:** establish correctly classified source curves and a coherent stern coordinate ledger first; correct the hull/deck/counter envelope next; then rebuild the rails, helm details, fittings and associated structure against that envelope. Moving the helm alone or applying another arbitrary shortening cut will not resolve the combined problem.

### 4.2 Counter location: slope is not enough

The present counter equation is:

```text
F(z) = HULL_AFT_FT + 1.8 × (reference_height − z)
reference_height = deck_height(HULL_AFT_FT) − deck_thickness
```

It is independent of X and uses an inherited overall aft coordinate as its surface anchor. That is insufficient evidence for a final across-ship counter/transom surface.

Using the **existing provisional S04 guide picks**, fixed longitudinal scale and one local deck anchor gives:

| Existing guide | Source F | Review Z | Current plane F at that Z | Source lies forward of current plane by |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Lower counter guide | +4.215 ft | 4.920 ft | +3.113 ft | **13.2″** |

**Interpretation:** these two controls are nearly parallel to the model's rake but displaced. The first correction fitted a plausible slope without establishing the correct surface location. **Qualification:** the previous guide labels, exact planked-edge identity and vertical anchor must be confirmed. This is an apparent offset diagnostic, **not an instruction to blindly translate every stern part forward by 13″**. ### R1 / R2 — hull, deck, counter and transom

**Required result:** the planked stern, deck edge, rounded/moulded shoulder and counter must form one coherent, source-registered envelope in plan, profile and aft section.

Remedy requirements:

1. Trace **separate named curves** for the actual wood deck edge, waterway/moulding outside edge, rail centres/edges, transom/counter profile, chine/rabbet and external fittings.
2. Record the native pixel points, source view, scale, F/X/Z transformation, curve identity, uncertainty and geometry condition for each control. Retain the unmodified source images.
3. Establish a surface anchor **independent of the overall bounding box**. Use explicit measured stern controls, not `HULL_AFT_FT` merely because it is currently the most-aft model reference.
4. Resolve whether the visible stern target is the surveyed arrangement/repair condition or the corrected lines. For the requested historic depiction, the recommendation is the **surveyed HAER arrangement and photographed repair state**, with S02's corrected lines retained as an independent numeric reference and the local difference documented.
5. Fit the aft planform and shoulder transitions from those curves. Use controlled transverse sections or a genuine surface representation where the reference demands across-ship curvature. A universal planar half-space is acceptable only if it independently meets all those controls.
6. Rebuild the local deck edge and moulding rather than retaining a full-width thin plate at a hardware/projection extent. Preserve actual required overhangs when the references establish them.
7. Preserve genuine internal space and normal planking thickness. Rebuild the **inner** counter/transom consistently with the outer surface; trimming only the exterior would reopen the hull or leave excess wood.
8. Refit connected frames, clamps, boxing, deck timbers and stem-post/keel interfaces. Do not shorten the visible body while leaving old floating structure inside it.
9. Preserve the source offset records and recorded-length witnesses. If the accepted visible wood bounds change, replace the implementation's old derived bound checks with controls for the newly verified physical boundary; do not distort the rest of the vessel to retain a mistaken overall extent.

The current `check_model_scale.py` also checks an inherited body span. Its **unit-scale** check remains valuable; its old body-span assertion must not become a circular reason to reject a better sourced aft boundary. Keep **304.8 mm per full-size foot** and the **15,240 mm 50′ reference** distinct from the final physical wood/fitting extents.
